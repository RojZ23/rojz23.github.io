About Breach Unit
Breach Unit is an independent investigative journalism initiative founded by Kira Daniels in 2014. Our mission is to uncover the truth behind the Nova Crest incident and the activities of Second Lives Corporation. We believe in transparency, accountability, and the public's right to know. We operate outside traditional media structures to avoid corporate and government influence. Our work is funded by anonymous donations and supported by a network of whistleblowers, researchers, and concerned citizens. If you have information related to Second Lives or the Nova Crest incident, you can contact us through secure channels. We protect our sources with the highest level of operational security. Remember: The truth doesn't fear investigation.
